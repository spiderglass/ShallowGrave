Volume I. April 2000.
April 2, 2000

It's entirely possible that I'm going to spontaneously combust at any given moment.

The dog is reading "If you can't live without me why aren't you dead yet?" by Cynthia Heimel. The cat waters the hydrangeias and bitches about the weather.

The bags are packed. The cell phone charged.

I'm running in twenty directions, have fifty things to do and am really in no mood for a week long business trip.

Bitch. Bitch. Moan. Groan.

On a happy, cheery note, I was 33% over my sales quota at work.

Hooray!

Yay!

Whoa.

So.

The phone rings. This time, I ignore it.

I really, REALLY am in no mood for this trip.

Yours,
Faith

PS - Something kinda funny happened last week at my doctors office. I'll share that upon my return, along with some other pressing gossip.
