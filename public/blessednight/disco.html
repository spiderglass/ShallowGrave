Discography

Violin (1997)

Track List:

  • 1. La Folia
  • 2. Sobre Canto Llano
  • 3. Largo
  • 4. Allegro
  • 5. Adagio
  • 6. Tambourin
  • 7. Willow
  • 8. Revelry
  • 9. On A Day...

Album Notes: Released by Messina productions, who don't seem to have any other releases to their name that I can find online. A very early demo CD of EA's classical recordings, featuring several of her original compositions written while she was a teenager. Only 100 copies were made.

Availability: Out of print

Track List:

  • 3. Largo
  • 4. Allegro
  • 5. Adagio
  • 6. Tambourin
  • 7. Willow
  • 8. Revelry
  • 9. On A Day...

Album Notes: Basically a re-release of Violin on EA's own record label, Traitor Records.

Availability: Out of print

Chambermaid (2001)

Track List:

  • 1. Chambermaid
  • 2. Chambermaid (Space Mountain Mix)
  • 3. Chambermaid (Decomposition Mix)
  • 4. Largo for Violin and Harpsicord
  • 5. What If
  • 6. What If (Blackbird Mix)
  • 7. Holllow Like My Soul
  • 8. I Don't Care Much (Cover)

Album notes:Released by Seraph Records. The Chambermaid EP is technically a single from the Enchant album. Almost all of the tracks from this EP are available on other CDs.

Availability: Out of print

By the Sword (2001)

Track List:

  • 1. By the Sword
  • 2. Castle Down
  • 3. Willow (Instrumental)
  • 4. I Know it's Over (Cover, video)

Album notes:Released on Traitor Records. All copies were signed, and all the proceeds were donated to Americares and the American Red Cross, to help survivors of 9/11.

Availability:Out of print

Enchant (2003)

Track List:

  • 3. Chambermaid
  • 4. Rupunzel
  • 5. Ever
  • 6. Second Hand Faith
  • 7. Juliet
  • 8. Remember
  • 9. Rose Red
  • 10. Castle Down
  • 11. Heard it All
  • 12. If You Feel Better
  • 13. Save You
  • 14. Epilogue: What If

Album notes: Enchant was first released in 2003 by EA's own label, Traitor Records, but it was also re-released by Trisol in 2007, in a standard and limited edition, again in 2008 in collaboration with Russian label Irond, and then it was re-released digitally in 2014 through Traitor Records.

Availability:CD: Out of Print. A high quality digital version is currently available on EA's website/The Asylum Emporium.
